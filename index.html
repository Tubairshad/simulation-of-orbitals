What is a node?

A node is a place where ψ = 0, so the electron is never found there. Radial nodes are spherical shells (n−l−1 of them). Angular nodes are planes or cones (l of them). In the cross-section view, white lines are nodes and the dark gaps around them are empty space. Nodal planes of p orbitals and some d and f orbitals are also drawn in the 3D view.
